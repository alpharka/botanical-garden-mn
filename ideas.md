# MenikahSudahDekat - Botanical Garden Theme

## Design Philosophy: Luxury Botanical Conservatory

The **Botanical Garden** theme transforms digital wedding invitations into an immersive botanical experience. Inspired by lush greenhouse environments, pressed botanical illustrations, and elegant garden architecture, this design celebrates the couple's love story within a serene, nature-inspired sanctuary.

---

## Design Movement & Core Principles

**Movement:** Contemporary Botanical Luxury — blending Victorian botanical illustration traditions with modern minimalism and organic design principles.

**Core Principles:**
1. **Photography as Primary** — Couple's images remain the visual anchor; botanical elements frame and enhance, never compete
2. **Organic Symmetry** — Asymmetric layouts that feel natural and breathable, avoiding rigid grids
3. **Airy Elegance** — Generous whitespace, soft depth, and medium visual density create a premium, unhurried experience
4. **Nature-Inspired Precision** — Botanical illustrations, pressed leaf motifs, and garden signage styling provide refined decoration

---

## Layout DNA

**Hero:** Centered vertical composition with botanical framing. Cover section dominates with couple photo as the focal point.

**Content Width:** Comfortable reading width (max 700px on desktop) with generous margins.

**Grid:** Single-column mobile, balanced multi-column desktop. Sections alternate image placement (left/right) for visual rhythm.

**Alignment:** Centered with intentional asymmetry. Stories and gallery use alternating layouts.

**Spacing:** Generous — minimum 3rem between major sections, 1.5rem between subsections.

**Hierarchy:** Photography → Typography → Decoration

---

## Signature Elements

1. **Botanical Dividers** — Curved SVG wave dividers resembling garden pathways between sections
2. **Pressed Leaf Borders** — Subtle leaf illustrations framing cards and portraits
3. **Engraved Garden Signage** — Event cards styled like elegant botanical venue markers
4. **Watercolor Foliage Accents** — Soft, organic background textures behind quotes and text
5. **Floral Illustration Overlays** — Hand-drawn style botanical elements (wildflowers, ferns, branches)

---

## Interaction Philosophy

**Buttons:** Rounded pill-shaped buttons with botanical outline, soft hover scale (1.02x), gentle press compression (0.98x).

**Cards:** Soft shadows (0 4px 12px rgba), subtle sage borders, rounded corners (12px).

**Forms:** Minimal design with generous padding, botanical focus states (sage green ring).

**Maps:** Embedded inside elegant framed cards with botanical corner accents.

**Gallery:** Editorial masonry layout with stagger reveal animations.

**Floating Elements:** Occasional drifting botanical illustrations (parallax effect on scroll).

---

## Animation Guidelines

**Motion Personality:** Organic, gentle, floating, story-driven.

**Section Transitions:**
- Fade, Fade Up, Soft Scale, Cross Fade, Gentle Parallax

**Text Animations:**
- Headings: Fade Up (300–500ms ease-out)
- Body: Fade (300–500ms ease-out)
- Quotes: Opacity reveal (600–900ms ease-out)

**Image Animations:**
- Slow fade (600–900ms ease-out)
- Soft scale (1.02x, 600–900ms ease-out)
- Gentle slide (50px offset, 600–900ms ease-out)
- Reveal mask (organic mask reveal, 800–1000ms ease-out)

**Gallery:** Stagger reveal (30–80ms between items)

**Stories:** Alternate slide directions (left/right)

**Events:** Cards fade upward, maps fade, buttons slide upward

**Buttons:** Hover soft scale (1.02x, 150–250ms), press compression (0.98x, 150–250ms)

**Accessibility:** Replace movement with opacity-only transitions when `prefers-reduced-motion` is enabled.

## Typography System

**Headings:** Elegant high-contrast serif (Playfair Display or similar)
- Display: 48px–64px, weight 700, letter-spacing +0.5%
- Section: 32px–40px, weight 700, letter-spacing +0.3%
- Card: 24px–28px, weight 600, letter-spacing +0.2%

**Body:** Readable humanist serif or refined sans-serif (Lora or Montserrat)
- Body: 16px–18px, weight 400, line-height 1.6
- Small: 14px–16px, weight 400, line-height 1.5

**Quotes:** Flowing script (Cormorant Garamond or similar)
- 20px–24px, weight 400, italic, letter-spacing +0.1%

**Letter Spacing:** Slightly expanded headings (+0.3% to +0.5%), normal body

**Line Height:** Comfortable and airy (1.6–1.8 for body, 1.4 for headings)

## Media Guidelines

**Photo Direction:**
- Natural lighting, warm tones, outdoor garden feeling
- Soft focus backgrounds, faces always unobstructed
- Editorial cropping, high sharpness, soft contrast
- No heavy filters, preserve skin tones

**Safe Areas:** Reserve space for faces, titles, primary CTAs, important text

**Gallery:** Masonry layout, lazy loading, preserve resolution, optional lightbox

**Videos:** Embedded YouTube only, responsive, thumbnail before playback

**Maps:** Responsive embedded Google Maps, never screenshots
